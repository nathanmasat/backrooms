# THE BACKROOMS: DON'T LOOK BACK

A first-person survival horror game that runs in the browser: 20 levels, plus a secret Level 20. It was made by Nathan.

**Play:** open `index.html` in a browser, or visit the GitHub Pages site for this repository.

## What's in this folder
| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `index.html` | The whole game: graphics, levels, monsters, phone, story, everything. A table of contents at the top lists every part in order. |
| `docs/FEATURE_CHECKLIST.md` | Every feature, marked done, partly done or not possible. |
| `docs/CHANGELOG.md` | The history of updates. |

## How to play
1. **Getting started:** press **PLAY** and pick a difficulty: CASUAL, NORMAL, NIGHTMARE or DON'T LOOK BACK.
2. **Each level:** find the items the level asks for, then reach the elevator.
3. **Staying alive:** stay quiet, hide in lockers, keep your flashlight charged and keep your sanity up.
4. **The story:** collect documents, recordings and VHS tapes to piece it together. You read and watch them on your phone.

## Controls (default keys)
| Key | Action | Key | Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| W A S D | Move | F | Use / open / hide / talk |
| Shift | Sprint | E | Pick up / stun gun |
| C | Crouch | R | Reload / flash |
| Z / X | Lean | T | Flashlight |
| Space | Hold breath (while hiding) | Q | Monster scanner |
| 7 | Change camera view | I | Inventory |
| M | Mini-map | P | Phone |
| B | Big map / drone map | O | Compass |
| L / N / G | Lighter / night vision / glow stick | 1–5 | Gadgets |
| Esc | Pause | Tab | Scoreboard |

You can change most keys in **SETTINGS → CONTROLS** on the main menu.

## Hide and Seek keys
- **Hider:** E transform (or H) · J prop wheel · [ ] switch prop · Z / X rotate · 1–4 abilities.
- **Seeker:** F or click to tag · Q Detection Pulse.
- Other players are AI, because there is no online server yet.

## Phone apps

- **Story & lore:** Lore Archive, VHS Archive, Found Tapes, Encyclopedia, Monsters, Secrets, Notes.
- **Progress:** Run, Daily, Trophies, Recorder.

## New players
- Your first Level 0 is easier: a guide arrow to the keycards and the elevator, glowing beams over keycards, a sleeping Boiled One for 45 seconds, then a slower one, and half-speed sanity and battery drain. Press H to turn it off. It ends after your first escape.

## Secrets
- **The Forgotten Security Office:** look for a door marked SECURITY on Level 0 or Level 5 and press E. Inside are security cameras (E at the security computer; 1–8 or ←/→ to switch, W/S to zoom, E to close) and a letter from Nathan.

## Notes
- **Saves:** progress is saved in your browser, with separate save slots.
- **Multiplayer:** the game has no online server, so multiplayer features are not available. Stalker mode uses AI teammates instead.
